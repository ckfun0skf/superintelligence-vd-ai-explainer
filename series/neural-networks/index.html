What is the most important thing inside AI? There isn’t one answer for every kind of AI. But one of the central engines behind modern generative AI is the neural network. The name can sound mysterious. A neural network is a mathematical system: numbers go in, calculations transform them, and numbers come out. Training changes the adjustable values inside those calculations. The idea begins with a simplified mathematical model inspired by a biological neuron. In 1943, Warren McCulloch and Walter Pitts described one such model. In 1958, Frank Rosenblatt introduced the perceptron, a simple system that could learn to separate patterns. These were not tiny copies of a brain. They were building blocks for a new kind of calculation. To see how those blocks work, let’s follow one number at a time.
